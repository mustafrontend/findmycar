Find My Car — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

🛡️ 100% Device-Side Privacy Guarantee: Find My Car does NOT track, store, sell, or transmit your location data, camera photos, or voice notes to any remote server. All data remains strictly on your device.

1. Information We Access & Collect

Find My Car accesses only the necessary permissions required to pinpoint your parked vehicle and guide you back:

  • Location Data (GPS): Used exclusively to pin your car's latitude and longitude coordinates and compute walking directions. Location data is never sent off your device.
  • Camera Access (Optional): Used solely when you take a photo of your parking column number or ticket. Photos are saved locally within your app's local storage.
  • Microphone Access (Optional): Used solely when you record a 5-second voice memo. Audio recordings are saved locally as encoded data on your device.

2. Data Storage & Security

We operate on a Zero Infrastructure Cost architecture. All your parking spots, photos, audio memos, and timer preferences are stored locally using Apple iOS CoreData / LocalStorage. We do not maintain any cloud servers, databases, or user accounts.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Find My Car does not contain third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or analytics trackers. When you tap "Open in Google Maps" or "Open in Apple Maps", the app redirects you to the respective maps application using standard system URL schemes.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is rated 4+ and does not collect any personal information from children or adults alike.
