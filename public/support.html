Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

🚗 How does Find My Car save my location?

Tap the big "BURAYA PARK ETTİM" (Park Here) button. The app uses high-precision GPS to store your exact latitude and longitude in under 1 second.

🧭 How do I navigate back to my vehicle?

Tap "ARABAMA GÖTÜR" (Take Me to Car). The app activates a 360° gyroscope compass pointing straight to your car and offers 1-tap walking directions on Apple Maps or Google Maps.

⏰ How do parking meter alerts work?

Set your parking duration (e.g. 60 mins). The app will trigger a loud alarm sound and send a local push notification 15 minutes before your time expires.
